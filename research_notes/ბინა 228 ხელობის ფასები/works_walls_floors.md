# Flat №228 — labour rates for wall, ceiling and floor works in Tbilisi (published 2025–2026, read on 2026-10-11)

Scope: labour rates per unit of work for 13 budget rows (`la-plaster` … `la-waste`). All figures are GEL as printed on the linked page. Georgian quotations are the page's own words. Pages relied on are saved in `SCRATCH/round3/pages/works_walls_floors/` (file names are given in the candidates file). The machine-readable rates are in `SCRATCH/round3/candidates/works_walls_floors.json` (54 entries; every evidence string was checked by script against the saved page).

Method limits that the reader must know: the WebSearch tool returned no Tbilisi results at all, neither for the Georgian queries nor for the three Russian ones (вывоз строительного мусора, шпаклевка/покраска, прайс на ремонтные работы); DuckDuckGo and Brave (through `curl`) answered about five queries in total and then blocked the address for the rest of the session. Most sources were therefore found by walking sitemaps and category pages of platforms (servisebi.ge, gurus.ge, serwish.ge, xelosnebi.ge, datvale.ge) and by probing about 150 likely domain names. No browser was used, so pages that render only with JavaScript (masteron.ge, servis.ge, remontonline.ge, mymarket.ge, Facebook) could not be read.

## 1. Which Tbilisi renovation companies publish a price list of works?

### Takeaway
Only one Tbilisi company was found that publishes a full line-by-line price list of works: Euroremonti.ge (calculator pages, 76 lines in 12 sections, undated, does not say whether materials are included). PrimeBuild publishes single rates inside service pages (laminate, skirting, painting, self-levelling). Every other company found publishes only a whole-flat price per m² or "price after inspection".

### Inferences
- Euroremonti's list is the only single source that covers 11 of the 13 rows, so it can serve as the "company" anchor of each row; because it is silent on materials, its figures should be shown with that caveat rather than as pure labour.
- The same company's package page says its package prices are "ხელობისა და შავი მასალების ჩათვლით" (labour plus rough materials). That makes it plausible, though not stated, that the calculator's unit rates also include rough materials — one more reason to ask before treating them as labour only.
- Euroremonti's rates sit at or above the top of craftsmen's ranges for plaster (30 and 20 against 9–20) and doors (200 against 120–130), which is consistent with a company price that carries overhead.

## 2. What do craftsmen platforms and directories state?

### Takeaway
Three kinds of platform figures exist: (a) individual craftsmen's ads with a stated tariff (servisebi.ge, gurus.ge, xelosnebi.ge) — the most concrete, but undated; (b) "typical price" sentences written by the platform itself (servisebi.ge landing pages, gurus.ge "ფასები" blocks, serwish.ge articles); (c) guide sites that quote averages (remontimartivad.ge, datvale.ge). Platform ranges are wide and their lower bounds are well below what individual Tbilisi ads ask.

### Inferences
- Individual Tbilisi ads cluster tightly: plaster 15 GEL/m² without materials, putty 30 GEL/m², painting 10 GEL/m², an interior door 120–130 GEL. The platforms' own ranges start much lower (plaster 5–6, putty 5, painting 3) and look like country-wide floor values, so the lower bound of a platform range is a weak "low" figure for Tbilisi.
- Platform ranges for the same work disagree with each other (painting+putty "მალიარკა": 8–20 on servisebi.ge, 18–22 on remontimartivad.ge, 30 in two Tbilisi ads, 25 at Euroremonti), which fits a real spread of roughly 18–30 GEL/m² for a full putty-and-paint job in Tbilisi.

## 5. Do sources price whole-flat "turnkey" finishing per m² of floor area?

### Takeaway
Yes. Labour-only (or labour plus rough materials) offers for a flat handed over as a "white frame" run from 280 to 700 GEL per m² of floor area; from a "black frame" 330–1,500 GEL. With all materials, guides put a full renovation at 500–1,100 GEL/m² for economy to standard class.

### Inferences
- For 62.7 m² of floor area (computed: rate × 62.7): Tbilremonti white frame 280 × 62.7 = 17,556 GEL, black frame 330 × 62.7 = 20,691 GEL; Proremonti white frame 300–700 → 18,810–43,890 GEL, cosmetic 150–350 → 9,405–21,945 GEL; PrimeBuild green frame 380 → 23,826 GEL, white frame 410 → 25,707 GEL, black frame 520 → 32,604 GEL (these three include rough materials); Evroremonti white frame 269 → 16,866.30 GEL if the figure is per m².
- remontimartivad.ge's example implies 8,000–11,000 GEL of craftsmen's pay for 50 m², i.e. 160–220 GEL per m² of floor area for a white-frame flat (computed: 8,000 ÷ 50 and 11,000 ÷ 50); at 62.7 m² that is about 10,032–13,794 GEL for all trades.
- These whole-flat figures cover every trade (electrics, plumbing, tiling, heating too), so they bound the sum of all labour rows of the budget, not just the thirteen rows of this note. The "typical" column of section 3 sums to roughly 15,600–16,200 GEL for the wall, ceiling and floor rows alone if every row has to be done, which is close to the cheapest turnkey offers; this suggests that either the putty and paint figures overlap (see section 3) or that per-unit company rates run higher than package prices.

- No source prices a finishing-only package for a flat whose rough works (plaster, screed, wiring) are already done, apart from the "white frame" and "cosmetic" bands above.
