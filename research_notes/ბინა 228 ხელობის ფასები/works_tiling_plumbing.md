# Labour rates for bathroom tiling and plumbing in Tbilisi (flat №228; pages read on 11 October 2026)

Method in one paragraph: Georgian and Russian searches (WebSearch, plus DuckDuckGo/Brave result pages fetched with `curl` until both rate-limited), then every page was downloaded with `curl` and read from the saved copy — no browser was started. Saved pages: `scratchpad/round3/pages/works_tiling_plumbing/` (168 files; text versions in `scratchpad/round3/scripts_tp/txt/`). Machine-readable rates: `scratchpad/round3/candidates/works_tiling_plumbing.json` (18 entries; every quoted wording was checked by script against the **visible** text of the saved page). A rate was accepted as a candidate only if (a) the page shows it to a visitor today, (b) the page states the unit, (c) the provider is in Tbilisi, (d) nothing on the page dates it before 2025. Article-style "average price" figures and expired ads are reported below but are not candidates. All prices are GEL, labour only unless said otherwise.

## 1. Which Tbilisi companies and craftsmen publish tiling and plumbing price lists?

### Takeaway
Very few do. One construction company (stroydom.ge) publishes a full per-m² / per-device list for tiling, waterproofing, plumbing and heating, dated 10 October 2026; one renovation company (euroremonti.ge) has an undated on-line calculator; four plumbing call-out services (santech.ge, santechservice.ge, NeoFix, smartplumber.ge) publish per-fixture tables; the rest are single craftsmen's ads or platform cards. Most Tbilisi tilers and plumbers advertise "ფასი შეთანხმებით" with no figure.

### Inferences
- stroydom.ge, santech.ge and santechservice.ge are template-built marketing sites: stroydom.ge shows the same "updated" date (10 October 2026, the day before reading) on every page, which suggests the date is refreshed automatically; santech.ge and santechservice.ge share one page layout but quote different figures. Their numbers are published and readable, but they are opening offers, not quotes.
- Because servisebi.ge keeps the old text of expired ads only in hidden tags, search engines still show "30/40/50 ლარი" snippets for pages where the owner would see no price. Any figure taken from a search snippet for that site must be re-opened before use.

### Gaps
- Facebook price posts could not be read (login wall for `curl`; no browser allowed).
- ss.ge / mymarket.ge service ads: the listing pages are built in the browser and returned no ad text to `curl`; not covered.
- xelosani.ru (two price pages that search results show) did not resolve (DNS failure); mastera-tbilisi.ge, mihouse.ge and myspec.ge returned 404 or empty pages.
- DuckDuckGo and Brave blocked automated queries after 2–3 searches each, so coverage rests mainly on the WebSearch tool's results.

## 2. Tiling: the base rate per m² and the stated surcharges (60×120 format, 45° mitred corners, niches, holes, epoxy grout)

### Takeaway
Published base rates for laying tile in a Tbilisi bathroom run from 35–55 ₾/m² (stroydom.ge) through 80 ₾/m² (euroremonti.ge calculator; Serwish platform "from 80") to "from 115 ₾/m²" (one master on VseMastera). Only stroydom.ge publishes any surcharge: large format (but defined as 80×160 cm and up) 70–110 ₾/m², 45° mitre 12–18 ₾ per running metre, epoxy grout plus 25–35 ₾/m². Nobody publishes a rate specifically for 60×120, for niches or for cutting holes.

## 3. Plumbing: rate per point for supply and waste; installation rate per fixture; concealed versus ordinary mixer; concealed cistern frame

### Takeaway
Companies price pipe runs per running metre (15–42 ₾/m supply, 10–32 ₾/m waste), craftsmen per point (50–80 ₾ in the two usable 2025–2026 sources; articles quote 90–110 ₾). Only stroydom.ge says chasing is included. A concealed mixer is priced by one source only (santech.ge, "from 150 ₾") — two to three times its rate for an ordinary mixer (50–80 ₾). A wall-hung toilet on a frame costs 150–250 ₾ to install against 80–100 ₾ for a floor-standing one. No source prices a rain-head shower set, a hygienic shower, a new linear drain or a tumble dryer connection as such.
