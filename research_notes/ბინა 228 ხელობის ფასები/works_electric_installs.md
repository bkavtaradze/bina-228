## 1. Which Tbilisi electricians, installers and renovation companies publish price lists?

### Takeaway
Eight sources publish itemised tariffs of their own (Profi Masters, electrik.ge, evroremonti.ge, technoshop.ge, mastera.ge, mastertbs.ge, karnizi.ge, metrix.ge); three more publish ranges per m² or per point (stroydom.ge, proremonti.ge, sess.ge). The rest are platforms and blogs with general "market" ranges, or sites with no prices at all.

### Inferences
- Two different price levels sit side by side: handyman call-out services (Profi Masters, mastera, mastertbs) price each item as a separate visit-sized job; renovation companies and electricians working by the point price far lower per item. For a whole flat the per-point and tariff-list rates are the relevant ones; the call-out rates mark the upper bound.
- electrik.ge's figures are several times lower than every dated 2026 list (socket 8 ₾ against 20–50 ₾). With "© 2024" in the footer the list is probably old; use it as the low bound only, with that warning.

### Gaps
- serwish.ge, servisebi.ge, servis.ge, craftnow.ge and Yandex Uslugi show their listings only with scripts running; without a browser nothing could be read, so individual craftsmen's ads on them are not covered.
- No dated Facebook price posts were reached (no search engine access to Facebook).
