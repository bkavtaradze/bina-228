# Kitchen appliances, sink, tap, worktops, cabinet makers and laundry pair — what can be bought in Georgia (Tbilisi), prices read on 10 October 2026

How to read these notes
- All prices are GEL as shown on the shop page on 10 Oct 2026. "A (was B)" = current price A, struck-through price B, i.e. A is a sale price. No "was" = no old price shown.
- megatechnica.ge listing pages print a row number in front of the price (row 1 "1 699" = 699 GEL). Every megatechnica price below was read with that in mind, and the ones used in packages were confirmed on the product page.
- ee.ge listing pages show "მარაგში" (in stock) or "მხოლოდ მაღაზიებში" (stores only); ee.ge product pages only show "check availability". megatechnica product pages show "out of stock" explicitly when it applies; otherwise only an add-to-cart button.
- Dimensions are quoted as the shop wrote them (H × W × D unless noted). Where a shop's figure looks like a typo it is flagged, not corrected.
- Design constraints checked against: fridge niche 56 cm wide (177–178 cm or 193–194 cm high class); wall recess 758 × 270 mm; hood to hide in a 500 mm (or 600 mm) wall cabinet; dishwasher 45 cm; laundry niche 610 mm wide × 660 mm deep; worktop 2.51 × 0.62 m and splashback 2.51 × 0.54 m (2.91 m² together).

### Inferences
- Niche check, depth 660 mm: a dryer needs space behind for the plug and (if drained) the hose and usually a door or front flush with the cabinet. Comfortable: Samsung 600, Midea 600, Bosch 613 (47–60 mm spare). Marginal: Electrolux 636/638 and Hisense DH3S902BB3 640 (20–24 mm spare) — only workable with an open-front niche and a flat plug. Do not fit: Gorenje 660, LG 660, Haier 665. Width 595–600 mm in a 610 mm niche leaves 5–7 mm each side for every model — tight but standard.
- Same-brand pairs, with what can actually be bought today: Samsung WW80FG3M05AWLP (480 deep) + DV90T5240AT/LP (600 deep) = 2,698 GEL, best depth fit, but no Samsung stacking kit found in Georgia; Bosch WLZ24210ME (515) + WQG1420AME (613) = 3,799.98, no Bosch kit found; Electrolux EW6F2281E (547) + EW7D394UJE (636) + kit E1WYHSK2 = 3,257, the only pair whose brand kit is on sale in the same shop, but the dryer depth is marginal; Hisense WF3S8043BB3 (510) + DH1I800UW2E (listed 510) = 1,948, cheapest, kit listed at 199 but out of stock, dryer depth to be verified; Midea MF01610US40/W + MD1180BH60/W = about 2,000, washer only 6 kg with no dimensions published.
- A stacking kit must match the pair's depths: the washer should not be shallower than the kit's lower limit. Pairing a 45–48 cm slim washer under a 60 cm-deep dryer (Samsung WW80FG3M05AWLP 480 under DV90T5240AT 600; Haier 480) gives a 12 cm overhang, which brand kits generally do not allow; the full-depth Samsung WW90DG5U34AELP (595) is the correct base for the Samsung dryer but is out of stock. This compatibility point comes from general kit practice, not from a Georgian page, and must be checked in the kit manual.
- Slim (≤ 48 cm) washers as a fallback: Electrolux EW6SM327DE 44.9 cm (out of stock), Samsung WW80AGAS21AELP 45 cm (out of stock), LG F2X5PYNY8 46 cm, Samsung WW80FG3M05AWLP 48 cm, Haier HW80-BP14929A-S 48 cm; Hisense WF3S8043BB3 (51 cm) and Bosch WLZ24210ME (51.5 cm) are mid-depth.

### Gaps
- No Samsung (SKK-type) or Bosch (WTZ-type) stacking kit was found in any readable Georgian shop; alta.ge and zoommer.ge could not be searched.
- Kit compatibility lists and depth ranges are absent from megatechnica's pages.
- Hisense DH1I800UW2E's listed 510 mm depth is unusual for a heat-pump dryer and is unverified; Beko B5T69233 and Midea MF01610US40/W have no dimensions on ee.ge; depths quoted by shops usually exclude the door and connections.
- megatechnica shows "out of stock" on several attractive units (Samsung WW90DG5U34AELP and WW80AGAS21AELP, LG pair, Hisense DH3S902BB3, Electrolux EW6SM327DE); restock dates are not given.
