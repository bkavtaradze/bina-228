# Bathroom and balcony for flat 228: tiles, sanitary ware and fittings that can be bought in Tbilisi (prices read 10 October 2026)

How to read these notes. Every price was read from the shop's own listing or product page on 10 October 2026, in GEL, with the unit the shop prints. "SALE" means the page shows a crossed-out higher price. Colour descriptions are my reading of the shop's product photo; where a hex value is given it is the average of the centre of that photo, so treat it as a rough guide (lighting and screens shift it), not a manufacturer value. The design target for the wall tile is about #CFC6BC. Tile quantities used later: terrazzo 4.5 m² floor + 5.5 m² shower wall = 10 m², walls 19 m², balcony 7.6 m²; packages add 10 % for cuts (11 / 21 / 8.4 m²).

## 1. Which terrazzo-look porcelain tiles are sold in Georgia (60 × 60, 60 × 120, 80 × 80) with warm chips, at what price, thickness and slip rating?

### Takeaway
Warm terrazzo exists in stock at ordinary DIY prices: about 31–43 GEL/m² at Domino and Gorgia (Ceroq Vibgyor 60 × 120, Itaca Gobi White 60 × 60, Ege Seramik Terrazzo White 60 × 60). The closest match to the brief by colour (off-white ground, fine beige and tan chips, matt, R9) is Kerama Marazzi "Terazzo" light cream 60 × 60, but its Georgian site shows no price. Most shops print no slip class at all, which matters for a wet shower floor.

### Inferences
- Best fit by colour and size for a floor plus one shower wall: (1) Kerama Marazzi Terazzo light cream 60 × 60 — the only one that is both the right warm off-white with small beige chips and carries a stated slip class (R9); weak point: no price online and 60 × 60 only, so more joints on the wall. (2) Domino Ceroq Vibgyor 60 × 120 — cheapest, warm, matt, and the same 60 × 120 format as the wall tile so joints can line up; weak points: a few navy chips, no slip class or thickness published. (3) Gorgia Ege Terrazzo White 60 × 60 — calmest and warmest "micro" terrazzo, 8 mm, on sale; weak point: chips are so fine that from two metres it reads as a plain warm white, and no slip class is shown.
- Itaca Gobi White (Domino) is warm overall but its teal chips pull it cooler than the brief; Itaca Neus Blanco has the right quiet chips but a grey, semi-polished ground — acceptable on the shower wall, doubtful on a wet floor.
- QUA Fonde Terrazzo has a good warm taupe tone but is fully polished: fine for the wall, wrong for the shower floor.
- Lima Terrazo Bej Antislip (and its grey sister Terrazo Gri) are the only terrazzo-named tiles sold explicitly as anti-slip; the Bej is greige rather than off-white, so it suits the balcony or a very calm floor more than the "terrazzo with visible chips" in the pictures.
- For 10 m² (11 m² with cuts) the material bill runs from about 341 GEL (Ceroq, 31.00) to 435 GEL (Ege, 39.51 sale) to 472 GEL (Itaca Neus Blanco, 42.90).

### Gaps
- Slip class (R-rating) is not published for any Domino, Gorgia (except the word "Antislip") or Nova terrazzo tile; it has to be asked for in store before using one on the shower floor.
- Thickness is not published for the Domino and Kerama Marazzi tiles.
- Kerama Marazzi Georgia shows no price or stock for Terazzo; a phone or showroom quote is needed.
- Modus.ge has a tile catalogue but loads products by script, so its listings could not be read; Ideal.ge shows tiles without prices. No 80 × 80 warm terrazzo was found anywhere; the only 80 × 80 terrazzo (Jaokeni Stracciatella) is high-contrast.
- Jaokeni's product pages print the price without a unit.

- By colour, the three closest to #CFC6BC are Ceroq Kaida Beige (Domino, 37.80), Geotiles Zero Taupe (Jaokeni, 89.00) and Santelmo Virgin Greige (Jaokeni, 66.00 on sale). - Slip class and thickness are missing for all Domino wall tiles.
- Colours were judged from shop photos only; a sample against the #CFC6BC swatch is still needed, and shade/calibre must match across boxes.
- Whether Jaokeni's sale stock covers 21 m² is not shown online.

- One-family route A (Roxy, DeRicci BG): concealed mixer + square head + arm + outlet holder + hand shower + hose, a DE-0322 / DE-0322I / DE-0422B wall basin mixer, a 12056K or DE-12057 hygienic set and a DES01 or DE02 drain in exactly 800 mm, plus matching hooks, paper holder and brush. It is the only route where the 80 cm gold drain is in the same range. Weak points: the ready-made DeRicci sets have round heads or no overhead head at all, so the square-head version must be assembled from parts and the shop should confirm the parts are compatible; the head size is not published; the brand is little known.
- One-family route B (Merlons, Empolo brushed gold): the M23 set already includes a 30 × 30 cm square head, and the wall basin mixer and hygienic set come in the same finish at lower prices. Weak points: no gold drain longer than 60 cm at Merlons (the drain would come from Roxy and may differ slightly in tone), and 30 cm is larger than the 25 cm drawn.
- Route C (Merlons, Meir brushed gold PVD) is the premium version of the same idea; weak points are price (2,407 GEL for the shower set alone) and round shapes.
- Mixing shops is where tone drifts: Roxy's Grassi hygienic shower already looks paler (champagne) than DeRicci BG in the shop photos, and "rose gold" lines (KFA Malaga, Empolo pink-gold) are visibly pinker than brass. The Geberit plate is a brass-coloured plastic, so it will be close to, not identical with, PVD metal.

### Inferences
- Economy build with a brass-coloured plate: Visam Slim 80 frame (259.00) + Visam Karina Matt Gold plate (55.00) + Victory TR-A1253W-A bowl with seat (299.00) = 613.00 GEL, all from Domino. Weak point: unbranded-tier cistern and a painted gold plate that will not match PVD brass closely.
- Mid build: Geberit Alpha Duofix 12 cm (430.00 at Roxy; 573.00 at Gorgia) + Geberit Alpha brass plate (160.00 at Roxy; 209.00 at Gorgia) + Roca Gap with soft-close seat (689.00 at Domino) = 1,279.00 GEL at the lower prices. Weak point: the bowl and frame come from two shops; the plate is brass-coloured plastic.
- All-Roxy build: Geberit Alpha Duofix (430.00) + brass plate (160.00) + DeRicci DRC-WH2105 (779.00) = 1,369.00 GEL. Weak point: the DeRicci page does not say whether a seat is included.
- The boxed Grohe and Ideal Standard sets save matching effort but ship with chrome plates; a brass Grohe plate was not found on sale, so they would force a white or chrome plate.
- A Geberit plate only fits its own cistern family (Alpha plates on Alpha cisterns, Sigma on Sigma), and Visam, Alcadrain and Bien plates likewise fit only their own frames — choose the plate first, then the frame.

### Gaps
- Whether each bowl includes a seat, and its outlet and fixing dimensions, is often not stated in the listing title; the DeRicci and Bocchi bowls need checking.
- No brass or gold plate was found for Grohe frames; gold Alcadrain plates at Roxy are marked not available.
- Roxy's Geberit plate page mixes the names Alpha01 and the code 115.055.QE.1 (Gorgia lists 115.035.QE.1 as Alpha01 and 115.055.QE.1 as Alpha30), so the exact plate model should be confirmed before ordering.

### Inferences
- Shower glass: the design needs about 1.81 m × 2.20 m = 4.0 m² of 8 mm glass plus two hinges, wall profiles or clamps, a stabiliser bar and a handle in brass. Smart Mirror's "from 1,200 GEL" is a starting figure for a walk-in panel, so a fixed panel plus a hinged door at 220 cm with brass fittings should be expected to come in above it; only a quote will settle it.
- Every ready-made screen is 190–200 cm high and is a fixed panel without a door; the brushed-gold Sonni corner enclosure (800 GEL on sale) is the only ready-made item with doors and gold hardware, but it is 90 × 90 cm and 20 cm lower than drawn.
- Vanity top: 160 × 50 cm is 0.8 m² of slab before the basin; with a quartz slab at 380–550 GEL the stone itself is a few hundred GEL (if Zodi's prices are per m²), and the cost will be dominated by fabricating the integrated basin, mitred edges and fitting. Quartz or porcelain slab can be mitred into a rectangular trough basin; a seamless moulded basin needs acrylic solid surface, for which no priced Georgian fabricator was found.
- No ready-made vanity reaches 160 cm; the widest found is 120 cm, and the one stone-topped 120 cm set appears to be black.
- Mirror: nothing ready-made is close to 135 × 105 cm; the largest common size is 90 × 120 cm at 299 GEL. A custom mirror is the only way to hit the drawn size.

### Gaps
- No Georgian source read gives a price per m² for 8 mm tempered glass, or a price for brass hinges and clamps; Smart Mirror's calculator runs in the browser and could not be read.
- No fabricator of Corian-type acrylic solid surface with published prices was found; metrix.ge is a general renovation contractor, mirrorcity.ge did not load, mirror.ge is a parked domain, and mylux.ge sells household mirrors but was not examined in detail.
- Zodi's quartz prices carry no unit on the page (per m² is the trade norm but must be confirmed), and Stone Age publishes none.
- No oak drawer-unit maker with prices was found; the joinery is a separate quote.
- skinali.ge and skinalidecor.ge are Tbilisi tempered-glass workshops (kitchen splashbacks) that might also cut shower glass; this was not confirmed.
