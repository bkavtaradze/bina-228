## Key question 1 — For each item, what is on sale in Georgia now that matches the SHAPE, the COLOUR and the SIZE?

### Takeaway
Georgian shops carry real, priced options for every item, but almost never the exact shape + colour + size together: the sofa, both bed types, the dining table, the terracotta pouf, the tall glass cabinet and the pebble coffee table have no ready-made match and are made-to-order jobs, while electronics, mattresses, rug, chairs, bedside pieces and balcony furniture can be bought off the shelf. Two sources dominate: JYSK (cheap, in stock, heavy sales, beige only) and Contempo / Kave Home (the right materials — bouclé, wool, paper cord, porcelain, cement — at 3–8 times the price and mostly with a 2-month wait).
