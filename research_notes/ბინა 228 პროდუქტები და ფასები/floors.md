- Zodi's six decors are probably Kronospan herringbone decors: the numbers 326 / 8155 / 8573 / 8633 match Kronospan decor codes, Zodi says "Germany", and Jaokeni lists Kronospan "K326 Sundance Oak" herringbone at the identical regular price of 65.00 ₾. ## True 45° chevron versus 90° herringbone: what exists, at what price, in stock or to order?

### Takeaway
There is no true chevron laminate or SPC in any Georgian shop checked: every small-plank laminate/SPC is 90° herringbone. A chevron look at laminate price exists only as a print on a wide board (Egger EL291F / EL203F at 44.09 ₾/m², Peli LE275 at 32.78, Ege Floor BG-94 grey at 25.90). ### Inferences
- The design pictures show a 45° chevron. On the Georgian market that picture can be had in three honest ways: (a) the real thing in oak at roughly 400-650 ₾/m² for material alone; (b) a 90° herringbone laminate in the right oak tone (52-84 ₾/m²), which reads as a "zig-zag" floor but with stepped, not pointed, joints; (c) an Egger chevron-print board (44 ₾/m²) which gives pointed V's but with no real joints between the "strips" and a visible repeat every 327 mm board.
- The Egger print's V-strips are printed much narrower than 10-12.5 cm (many strips fit across a 327 mm board in the shop photo), so it will look finer-grained than the design drawing.
- "In stock" for the oak floors means "call us" (Glimtrex) or a badge (Belle Maison). For 64-67 m² of one batch the owner should assume a confirmation call and possibly an order from Austria.

### Gaps
- No lead time for ordering chevron from Austria (Austriawood, Scheucher) is published.
- No chevron laminate with separate small planks was found; I cannot rule out one in a showroom that has no web catalogue.
- The Egger decor names for EL291F / EL203F and their finish (matt or not) are not on Gorgia's page.

## Which products are closest to natural light oak, matt, with planks about 10-12.5 cm wide? Best ten, cheap laminate to engineered wood, with each one's weak point

### Takeaway
Ten floors fit the brief well enough to be worth seeing as samples. On colour and size together the strongest are Kronospan K326 Sundance Oak (126 mm, just at the top of the width band, 52 ₾ on sale at Jaokeni), Nova L8619 (101 mm, 35.90 ₾), Classen Ville+ Prades and Cetina (131 mm, 76 ₾ on sale) and, if the budget allows real wood, FinishParkiet Oak Nature herringbone (120 mm, 308 ₾ at Jaokeni) or the Austriawood 45° chevron in Oak Zillertal Natural (100 mm, 649 ₾/m²), which is the only product that is literally the floor in the design.

### Inferences
- If the floor must "sit close to natural oak veneer", the honey-beige decors with little grey (K326 Sundance, L8619, Ville+ Prades, EL291F, Oak Zillertal Natural) are the safest; Cetina, Zodi 450 and Flooreo Angel drift pale/yellow, and Victoria / Almagro drift towards brown-olive.
- Only two laminate-format options sit in or at the 10-12.5 cm width band: 126 mm (Kronospan, 1 mm over the top) and 101 mm (Nova / Modus 606×101); in real oak, FinishParkiet (120), Austriawood herringbone (122) and Austriawood chevron (100) do. Classen (131), Flooreo and the Georgian-made 694×132 (132) and Alsafloor (143) are progressively wider and will show a coarser zig-zag in the 8.8 m² hall.
- "Matt" is only actually claimed by Domino (Ville HB 8 mm "matt"; Ville+ "semi-matt") and by the oiled oak floors. Everything else needs to be judged from a sample.

### Inferences
- A printed chevron/herringbone board is laid like an ordinary plank, so 5-7% waste is enough: the Egger EL291F would then be about 2,700 ₾ (58.3 × 1.05 × 44.09 = 2,698.97).
- Real herringbone in four separate rooms with door thresholds usually needs the higher allowance; budgeting at +15% is the safer of the two columns. Chevron in solid or engineered oak is normally bought with 10-15% as well, and with A/B (left/right) boards in equal quantities.
- At Domino the 8 mm Classen decors cannot cover the flat from the stock shown (3.72-22.67), whatever the price.

- Pack sizes are not published for anything at Jaokeni (Kronospan, Alsafloor, Georgian-made, FinishParkiet), Zodi, Flooreo (Gorgia), Nova 606×101, Modus, parketi.ge / Glimtrex or Belle Maison, so those rows are not rounded to packs; real totals will be slightly higher.

- Delivery charges for Jaokeni, Zodi, Nova, Modus and the parquet shops were not collected.

- No Tbilisi fitter publishes a specific per-m² rate for herringbone laminate, herringbone parquet or chevron parquet; all pattern figures above are derived. Three phone quotes are needed.
- The flat's wall perimeter was not given, so total skirting length and cost cannot be computed.
- Nova's skirting and underlay categories were not read.
- The Domino Super Profil and VOX skirtings may be polymer rather than MDF (material is not in the listing title); "MDF" is confirmed only for the Gorgia Ladix and Modus AGT items.
