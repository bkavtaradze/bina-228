## 1. Does a recessed TWIN adjustable downlight exist in Georgian shops? Which models, price, cut-out? (items 1–3: twin downlights, single round spots incl. IP44, recessed linear lights)

### Takeaway
Yes. Recessed twin GU10 frames (one rectangular trim, two tilting lamps) are stocked in Tbilisi at three shops: newlight.ge (all-white flat frame F032-WH at 20 GEL; white 208 × 107 mm model A1382 at 74 GEL; 235 × 130 mm A0622 at 102 GEL), lum.ge (25–30 GEL, white or black, outer 173 × 90 mm and 183 × 110 mm) and domino.com.ge (V-TAC, 175 × 85 mm, 35.75 GEL). All are sold as empty frames, so each needs two GU10 lamps (5–10 GEL each); no page states the cut-out, only outer sizes, and the smallest frame that would cover the designer's 190 × 100 mm cut-out is newlight's A1382 (208 × 107 mm outer); A0622 (235 × 130 mm) also covers it but is larger. A ready-made trimless recessed linear light of 50–60 cm was not found in any scanned shop; the stocked routes are a recessed aluminium profile with strip, short multi-cell recessed "grille" lights (23–30 cm), or linear modules on a magnetic track.

### Inferences
- The designer's "one white rectangular trim holding two adjustable lamps" is a standard import item here: newlight F032-WH and lum.ge 6006-2-WH are the literal all-white matches; newlight A1382 (208 × 107 mm outer) is the smallest frame that would cover a 190 × 100 mm cut-out; A0622 (235 × 130 mm) also covers it but is larger. The lum.ge and V-TAC frames (173 × 90, 175 × 85, 183 × 110 outer) are smaller than that cut-out, so the drawing's cut-out must be re-dimensioned to the chosen frame before the ceiling is cut.
- Budget for one twin position = frame + 2 lamps: 20 + 2×7 = 34 GEL (newlight F032-WH + FSL 7 W 3000K), 25 + 2×7 = 39 GEL (lum.ge 6006-2), 74 + 2×10 = 94 GEL (newlight A1382 + 12 W lamps); 14 positions ≈ 476 / 546 / 1,316 GEL.
- CRI 90+ cannot be confirmed for any GU10 lamp found: no lamp page states CRI, so the "CRI 90+ preferred" wish has to be met by asking the shop for a specific high-CRI lamp.
- For the kitchen counter, three 60 cm pieces of recessed profile with a 24 V strip (profile ≈ 10–35 GEL/m, see section 4) is cheaper and closer to "slim white frame" than any ready-made fixture found.

### Gaps
- Cut-out dimensions are not published for any twin frame; only outer dimensions. Needs the shop or the box.
- lum.ge 6006-2-WH stock is ambiguous on the page (status "in stock" plus note "მარაგში არა").
- No trimless (plaster-in) twin was found, and the only integrated-LED 3000K twin (newlight GL7117-2/12W, black, 270 GEL) does not state CRI.
- No ready-made recessed linear fixture of 50–60 cm at 3000K was found in the scanned shops.
- IP44 recessed spots at 3000K: four models found (newlight DL73027-3.5 and SL74091-3 with CRI > 90; lum.ge 5376-5-5W-WH; Kanlux MARIN frame at Domino).

### Inferences
- Parts should be treated as compatible only inside one series: track widths differ (BRILLOP 4.5 mm slot, lum.ge Series 20 modules 22 mm wide, Ultra 26 mm, Gabis track 26 mm, AIMON 27–30 mm, newlight UT25 20.5 mm, GD-48V 28–30 mm), and shops file power supplies and connectors under the series name. This is an inference from the listed dimensions, not a statement on any page.
- "lum.ge Series 20" lists "Osram" as the brand of the LED modules; that most likely refers to the LED chips, not to an Osram-made track system.
- For the design (black, 48 V, warm 3000K, CRI 90+), only three lines state CRI > 90 on the page: lum.ge Ultra, newlight.ge GD20xx modules, and Gabis at Domino.

### Gaps
- No page states compatibility between brands or series.
- Colour temperature and CRI are missing on newlight.ge UT25 and M203 module pages.
- Power supplies for Gabis and AIMON tracks were not found on Domino's listing; BRILLOP feed-in connectors were not found either.
- Black versions of the lum.ge Series 20 30 cm diffuser module are not confirmed (codes end in "WH" and photos are white/grey).

### Inferences
- For the dining table the realistic choices are (a) accept a smaller glass shade (lum.ge Ø 27 cm at 110 GEL is the largest found), or (b) order the specified Ø 30–40 cm clear dome from a showroom or abroad — the online catalogues of the mass-market shops do not carry it.
- The brass tone on these fittings is described on the pages only as "ოქროსფერი" (gold) or "თითბერისფერი" (brass colour); none states "brushed brass", so finish match to the other brushed-brass metal must be checked in the showroom.
- Wall-light globe diameters are mostly unstated; only newlight QH-B2567 (10 cm) and lum.ge 2723-1W (15 cm) give a figure.

### Gaps
- No clear glass dome pendant Ø 30–40 cm with brass fitting found; newlight.ge chandeliers above 460 GEL and lum.ge "Luxury" and "new collection" categories were not checked.
- JYSK KORNELIUS stock was shown only for Batumi and Kutaisi stores; Tbilisi store stock not shown.
- Globe diameters missing on most wall-light pages; switch presence is judged from photos, not stated in text.
- gorgia.ge and modus.ge decorative ranges were scanned by title only and contain no close match (budget spot-type wall lamps and generic LED chandeliers).

## 4. LED strip quality tiers and prices per metre, profile prices per metre, driver prices; a worked total for 44 m (item 8)

### Takeaway
24 V 3000K strip is stocked at lum.ge (5 GEL/m for a basic 5 mm strip, 12 GEL/m for a dot-free COB strip at 14 W/m, both sold as 5 m rolls), domino.com.ge (Guoye 10 W/m, 17 GEL/m in 10 m rolls) and newlight.ge (9.50–34 GEL as listed, unit not stated on the page); no strip page in any shop states CRI, so CRI 90+ cannot be confirmed from the listings. Aluminium profiles cost about 7–9 GEL/m (slim surface/recessed), 22–40 GEL/m (wide recessed) with corner profile only at newlight.ge (about 6.30 GEL/m with diffuser); 24 V drivers cost 30–128 GEL (60–500 W). A complete 44 m job (strip + profile + drivers) works out at roughly 750 GEL (basic) to 1,225 GEL (COB) at lum.ge prices.
