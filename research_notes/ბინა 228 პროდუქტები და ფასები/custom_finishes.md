# Made-to-order joinery and finishing materials in Tbilisi — price guide for flat 228 (researched 10 October 2026)

How to read the price labels used below:
- **published rate** = a per-unit price printed on the maker's or shop's own page;
- **listing** = the price of one specific product or advert;
- **guide** = a figure from a consumer article, not from a seller;
- **snippet** = seen only in a search-result summary, page itself could not be opened (treat as unverified);
- **my arithmetic** = a total or per-litre / per-metre figure I calculated from the cited numbers.
All prices are GEL. "Sale" means the page showed a struck-through higher price on the day.

Method limits that affect every section: Georgian-language web search returned mostly noise, so the notes rely on shop and maker pages that could be opened directly. Facebook / Instagram price posts were not checked. Several maker sites did not resolve at all (bdesigne.ge, artico.ge, mirrorcity.ge, myfardebi.ge).

## 1. What do Tbilisi cabinet makers charge per m² for painted-MDF built-ins and oak-veneered fronts, and for kitchens? (items 1, 2, 3, 5)

Almost no maker publishes what the rate includes, and none publishes a per-running-metre kitchen rate — kitchens are also quoted per m² or per example.

### Inferences
- **Wardrobes, 15.5 m² of fronts (5.5 + 4.5 + 5.5), my arithmetic:** Genabuild at 950 GEL/m² = about 14,700 GEL; Home is. guide rates of 700–850 GEL/m² = about 10,900–13,200 GEL; a laminate build at TrueHomeBuild's 350 GEL/m² entry rate = about 5,400 GEL but that is not painted MDF (Defo's "from 350 GEL per m²" gives no material, so it cannot be read as a painted or veneered rate either). TrueHomeBuild's own kitchen example prices painted MDF at roughly twice laminate (2,500 vs 1,200), which is consistent with 700+ GEL/m² for painted fronts.
- The per-m² rate normally covers carcass, fronts and standard fittings; the 2022 worked example shows drawers, premium runners, lighting, delivery and stairs-carrying charged extra. For wardrobes to 2.98 m, ask each maker whether the rate applies to the full front height (many sheets are 2.80 m, so a separate top row of doors is likely) and whether 66 cm depth costs more than 60 cm.
- **Fit to the design.** Colour: painted MDF can be sprayed to a code, so give the maker a code rather than "greige" — computed nearest codes to #C1B4A6 are NCS S 2005-Y50R, Tikkurila V484 / J485, Farrow & Ball Jitney 293; RAL Classic has nothing close (RAL 7044 Silk grey is the nearest and is visibly greyer) — see section 3 for sources and method. Material: Genabuild and Litten Tree explicitly sell painted MDF; Defo offers both veneer and painted; TrueHomeBuild's headline rate is laminate, so its painted price must be requested. Size: nobody states a maximum door height.
- **Weak points.** Genabuild: no stated inclusions and no phone on the site. Litten Tree: published rates are for kitchens, wardrobe rate must be asked. Defo: "from" prices only, site looks unmaintained (broken security certificate). TrueHomeBuild: Russian-language site dated 2025, painted-MDF and veneer rates not published. Home is.: articles, not offers.
- **Oak-veneer pieces (TV console, display cabinet, kitchen upper row, fridge housing).** Only guide rates exist: veneered MDF from 850–900 GEL/m². By formula (my arithmetic) the TV console front is 2.69 × 0.36 = 0.97 m² and the display cabinet 0.54 × 2.60 = 1.40 m², i.e. roughly 850 and 1,250 GEL at 870–900 GEL/m², but the m² formula understates small complex pieces: TrueHomeBuild's minimum for a living-room unit is already 900 GEL in laminate, and the rounded end, four drawers, concealed wall fixing, black metal frame, fluted glass and LED are all outside any published rate.

### Gaps
- No Tbilisi maker publishes a per-running-metre kitchen price; the market quotes per m² of front or per example.
- No published price for oak-veneered fronts from a named maker (only the Home is. guide figures and Defo's "veneer or painted" at unspecified rates).
- No published prices for the one-off pieces: floating oak TV console with rounded end, metal-framed fluted-glass display cabinet with LED, 259 cm white desk with pedestal, floating walnut shelves, lit shelf niche, rounded walnut hall shelf. These need written quotes.
- bdesigne.ge appears in search results with a price table (kitchen laminate about 395–460, acrylic / MDF about 520, unit unreadable) but the site would not open, so the figures are unverified.

### Inferences
- **Three-door budget, my arithmetic:** two oak-veneer flush doors at 1,300–1,400 = 2,600–2,800; one veneered hidden door 1,495; three black handles 120–380; three magnetic latches 108; fitting 150 per ordinary door and 100–500 for the hidden one = 400–800. Total about 4,700–5,600 GEL, excluding the oak panels above the doors.
- **Fit to the design.** Colour / material: only Genabuild names oak; Exposhop's two veneered doors say "natural veneer" and one is described as brown, so the species and tone must be confirmed against the #B08A62 oak sample, and all three doors plus the overpanels should come from one veneer batch. Size: Exposhop's stock leaves are 215–216 cm high and Domino's 215 cm; the brief allows 210–220 cm, so they fit, but a door-plus-panel to 2.98 m needs made-to-order work (Rinox states to-ceiling heights to order; Genabuild states "any size").
- **Weak points.** Genabuild: cheapest named oak door but nothing stated about frame, hinges or fitting. Exposhop veneered hidden door: imported stock size only, veneer species unstated, latch and handle extra. Domino Terminus Invisible: most complete kit and in stock, but it is an MDF block whose surface finish is not stated on the page (it appears to be meant for painting), so oak cladding would be an extra joinery job, and stock is 2–5 pieces. ProfilDoors Leon at Domino: only 2.00 m high and PVC-faced — wrong for this design. Rinox: price is a range on an old page. Gorgia: not veneer.
- The practical route for "invisible door clad in oak" is either the Exposhop veneered hidden door (if the veneer matches) or a paint-grade hidden kit (749–1,268 GEL) clad by the same joiner who makes the oak wall panels, so grain and colour run through.

### Gaps
- No price found for an oak-veneered overpanel / transom to the ceiling from any supplier.
- No Georgian dealer or price found for Sofia, Volkhovets or Union doors; Profildoors Georgia and Coridoor exist but publish no prices.
- Installation price for a concealed-frame door is only available as Rinox's 100–500 GEL range; note the frame must be built in before plastering.

## 3. Paint: which brands are tinted in Tbilisi, price per litre, and the closest catalogue colours to the three hex values (item 7)

### Takeaway
Caparol (machine-tinted in Tbilisi to NCS, RAL and its own 3D fan), Tikkurila and Hempel tintable bases at Domino, and Marshall at Gorgia cover the range from about 11 GEL to 85 GEL per litre; 30–40 litres costs roughly 350–600 GEL in an economy matt, 550–1,050 GEL mid-range and 950–1,700 GEL in Tikkurila / Hempel premium lines. **Closest catalogue colours (computed, not from the brands' own matching tools)**
Method: the three hex values were compared by CIEDE2000 colour difference (dE; under about 1 is hard to see, 2–3 is a visible but small shift, over 5 is a different colour) against open colour tables: NCS S 1950 and RAL from [python-ncs data](https://github.com/5monkeys/python-ncs), Tikkurila Symphony codes scraped from tikkurila.pl in [tikkurila-colors](https://github.com/programmer0121/tikkurila-colors), and RAL Classic, Benjamin Moore and Farrow & Ball from [colornerd](https://github.com/jpederson/colornerd). Screen values of real paints are approximations, and the two RAL tables disagree with each other by several dE.

### Inferences
- **Skirting fit.** Domino's Super Profil 80 mm and 95–99 mm white boards match the 8–10 cm brief at the lowest verified price; the listing does not say whether they are paintable, which matters if the skirting is to be wall-colour rather than white. Genabuild's painted plinth can presumably be sprayed to the wall or joinery colour together with the cabinets and doors, but its height is not stated and two of the named profiles are grey.
- **Radiator fit.** Hall: the ECA 600 × 400 mm panel is the exact size asked for and the cheapest; it is a plain ribbed-front panel, and the page does not state colour (panel radiators of this type are normally white). Beside the floor-length window: the Bauger 1800 × 500 mm vertical panel (550 GEL) is the plain option; the Avonflow CL03 (349 GEL, 245 mm wide) is the slimmest and cheapest white vertical; Warma, Lojimax and Ridea models are flat-tube designer pieces at 3–4 times the price.
- **Weak points.** None of the listings states heat output, so sizes cannot be checked against the rooms' heat demand; the Thermocenter page does not say which dimension is height for the 600-series options; Gorgia prices are sale prices.

### Gaps
- No classic multi-column tubular steel radiator (the "column radiator" look) was identified by name and price in Georgian catalogues; Thermocenter's 273-item decorative range was only read on its first page.
- Domino's general radiator and skirting category pages returned server errors; only the MDF-skirting sub-page opened. A snippet from Modus.ge showed an AGT MDF skirting 16 × 80 × 2800 mm matt white at 13.90 per piece and a 100 mm paintable one at 15.90 (currency not shown) — unverified.
- Total metres of skirting needed are not known to me, so no total is given.
