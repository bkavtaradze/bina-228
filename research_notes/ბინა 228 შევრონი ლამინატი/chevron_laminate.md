# Chevron laminate in Georgia — flat 228, checked 16 October 2026

Owner's request: laminate laid as chevron (ends cut at an angle, pointed "V", as in the designer's pictures), colour close to the design (warm natural medium oak, matt; not pale, not grey).

## Takeaway
No laminate made of separate angle-cut chevron planks is sold in any Georgian shop checked. In laminate the chevron exists only as a pattern printed on a wide board, laid like an ordinary plank (so 10% waste is enough: 64.4 m² instead of 67.3). True chevron planks exist only in real oak (330–650 ₾/m², rejected by the owner on 2026-10-10).
