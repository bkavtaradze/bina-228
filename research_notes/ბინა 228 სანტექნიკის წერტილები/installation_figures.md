## 1. Geberit Duofix 111.950.00.6
- Identity confirmed: 111.950.00.6 = Duofix element for wall-hung WC, 112 cm, Sigma 12 cm cistern, 50 cm wide, 112 cm high, replaces 111.300.00.5 (so it is the same product line, not a different one). Catalogue: Water supply R 1/2" with angle stop valve; 90-degree PE-HD connection bend o90 mm + adaptor socket o90/110 mm; large flush 4/4.5/6/7.5 l, small 2-4 l; actuator plate sold separately.
  https://catalog.geberit-global.com/en-XC/product/PRO_4081335
- Catalogue page gives NO depth, water-connection height, bolt distance or WC height values (stated "not listed").
- Dimension drawing (Geberit Duofix installation manual 966.027.00.0 (02), 12-2018; drawing for 112 cm / Sigma 12 cm / R 1/2"; NOT verified that this is the exact document number shipped with 111.950.00.6): https://cdn.data.geberit.com/documents/966.027.00.0_02.pdf (page 2)
  - Width 50; height 112 (cm). Feet adjust 0-20 cm.
  - Cold water R 1/2": drawn at top centre of frame, dimension 104.5 cm above finished floor (0 datum); 12 cm (unlabelled) from the rear datum shown in side view.
  - WC fixing axis: "23" cm above finished floor (marked at the bolt rail).
  - Bolt spacing: drawn "18/23" cm = 180 or 230 mm (inferred from drawing; matches UK retailer text "180 or 230 mm").
  - WC bowl top/seat height: "41 +2/-1" cm (= 40-43 cm) on the drawing; WC projection max "<= 62" cm.
  - Flush-plate datum: "100" cm above finished floor, drawn at the actuation opening (line meets the middle of the opening; inferred centre). Water connection 104.5.
  - Drain: bend o90 mm (adaptor 90/110 included); drawing dimensions at floor "4.8" and "9-13.5" cm, and page 11 shows bend positions "9-12" / "10.5-13.5" cm (adjustable). Axis offset from wall/frame not clearly labelled - confirm on site.
  - Depth: for installation in front of a solid wall drawing p.3 gives "13.5-20" cm (wall to front of frame); for drywall prewall p.5 ">= 13.5" cm. Wall/plate cladding thickness zoom on p.2: "2-6" cm. So minimum box depth = 13.5 cm + cladding (about 2-6 cm incl. tile, inferred) = ~15.5-19.5 cm minimum; frame itself reaches ~20 cm when set at max. (inferred sum)
  - Plate cut-out for Sigma20 not checked (p.16 shows tolerance "<= 5 mm" for Sigma01-type plates, "0 mm" for Sigma60).
  - Sources: same PDF pages 2, 3, 5, 11, 16.

## 4. Samsung WW80FG3M05AWLP and DV90T5240AT/LP
- Washer (manual for WW90*G3M0***/WW80*G3M0***/WW70*G3M0***, 02-2025; covers WW80FG3M05**): https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/media.markselectrical.co.uk/manuals/WW90FG3M05AWEU.pdf
  - Drain: "must be placed between 60 cm and 90 cm (*) above from the floor" (wash basin edge); drain pipe "between 60 cm and 90 cm high. It is advisable to use a 65 cm high vertical pipe"; insert hose 15 cm into pipe; standpipe min diameter 5 cm, min 60 l/min; sink branch "must be above the sink siphon so that the end of the hose is at least 60 cm (*) above the ground".
  - Inlet hose: manual says "Water taps must be within 120 cm from the rear of the washing machine so that the provided inlet hoses reach"; hose length not stated otherwise.
  - Tap thread: manual does not state the thread (only "threaded water tap" with rubber washer). Standard is G3/4" (my general knowledge, not from the doc).
  - Size WW80FG3M05**: W 595, H 850, D 480 mm (D with door/handle 520).
  - Clearances: sides 25, top 25, rear 50, front 550 mm.
- Dryer (manual DV9*T*/DV8*T*, https://www.skmagic.com.my/usermanual/Dryer%20User%20Manual_DV90T.pdf, covers DV9*T5*****):
  - Condensate: "strongly recommend connecting the drain hose to your drainage system"; built-in tank only if you cannot; factory default is the tank. Long drain hose 2 m supplied. Hose end "lower than 90 cm (*) from the bottom of dryer". "Do not extend the drain hose." Freezing note.
  - Size DV9*T5: W 600, H 850, D 650 mm. Stacking requires Samsung stacking kit (washer-model specific); alcove: sides 25, top 25, rear 50, front 490 mm; with washer, front opening >= 550 mm.
  - Niche 61 x 66: dryer 650 deep fits only with ~10 mm; washer 480/520 fits; check stack height 850+850+kit (~1.7 m+).
