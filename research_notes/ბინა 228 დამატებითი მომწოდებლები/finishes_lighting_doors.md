## Key question 1 — What do the m² Club Card partners carry for each item, at what price, with what discount; who publishes prices online; do they state how the discount works?

### Takeaway
Of the 18 partners named, eight publish usable prices online (lum.ge, newlight.ge, Brand R, Parkett Studio, Gabuild, Zodi, Nova, Lego Room — plus Gorgia, already in the budget); the rest are showroom-only or have no working catalogue (Elmont, Flügger, Art-Decor+, Room Design, New Design, Union, New Doors, Door Gallery, Praktika). None of the partner sites says anything about the card or how the discount is applied; the only stated condition is on m2.ge ("on presenting the card") plus the exclusions in the New Light entry.
