# Living-room and dining furniture for flat 228 — second round: m² Club Card partners and sellers the first round never looked at (Tbilisi, read on 10 October 2026)

How to read this file. Every price below was read on 10 October 2026 from the product's own page or from the shop's own open data feed, in GEL. "Club" is the discount the m² partner list states for that shop (source for all of them: [m² Club Card page](https://m2.ge/club-card), as saved in `m2_club_partners.md`); it is **not** applied to any price here. Saved pages are in `SCRATCH/round2/pages/furniture_living/`; the 31 proposed options are in `SCRATCH/round2/candidates/furniture_living.json`, and every one of their links was opened once more at the end (all returned the page, and all but the Chillout variants show the quoted price in the page as served — see the Chillout note).

Shapes and colours were judged against the designer's pictures (`designer/`): a low camel velvet sofa built of rounded tufted "bubble" blocks with a matching tufted pouf at its end; a white, brown-veined marble-look dining top with rounded corners on thick fluted oak legs; cream-framed wishbone chairs with woven seats; a pebble-shaped pale stone coffee table on a low dark wooden base (one picture shows a rectangular marble one instead); a floor-to-ceiling display cabinet with a thin black frame, fluted glass and oak inside; a plain warm-beige flat rug; a fiddle-leaf fig in a terrazzo pot.

## Key question 1 — What do the m² Club Card partners carry for each item, at what price, and which of them publish prices online?

### Takeaway
Of the sixteen partners named in the task, five publish prices that can be read (Comforter, Chillout, Marco, Ashley, Interiorismo) plus Coincasa and the maker Kave; Comforter is by far the richest source and has real candidates for the sofa, pouf, coffee table, dining table and display cabinet, all currently marked −30 % or more. Vibrant Home and Eurostyle — the two shops the owner named — publish no prices at all (catalogue only), and KARE / Laboratory My Home, Livin, Monkey Forest, Verona, Room Design, Enza Home could not be read or show no prices.
