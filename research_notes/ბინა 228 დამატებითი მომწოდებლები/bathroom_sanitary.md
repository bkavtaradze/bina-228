# Bathroom tiles, sanitary ware, brass fittings and radiators at shops the first round never looked at (flat №228, Tbilisi, read on 10 October 2026)

Method in one paragraph: every price below was read today from the product's own page (saved copies in `scratchpad/round2/pages/bathroom_sanitary/`), no browser was started. Finish (matt / glossy) and what a set contains were checked on the shops' own product pictures where the page text says only „ოქროსფერი“. Card discounts are quoted from the m² partner list and are NOT applied to any price. The machine-readable options are in `scratchpad/round2/candidates/bathroom_sanitary.json`.

## 1. What do the m² Club Card partners carry for each item, at what price, with what card discount — and which of them publish prices online at all?

### Takeaway
Of the seventeen partners named, eleven show GEL prices online (Deutsches Haus, VitrA, Lussoni, Cavoli, Veneto Design — whose shop, veneto.ge, is not on the partner list — Nova, Gorgia, Thermocenter, Celsiusi, Zodi and Merlons); Mosaics prices in euro, with a switch that shows the same figure in lari at the day's official rate; Ideal, Gallery Demasi and New Design are catalogues without a single price; Lego Room and Praktika have no site. The strongest new finds are two matt-gold concealed families at partner shops — Deante at Deutsches Haus (shower 1,450 + basin mixer 650 + hygienic shower 610 ₾, card 20% on all plumbing) and Mirtak at Veneto Design (shower 1,350 + basin mixer 785 ₾, card 15–30% on a selected collection) — while an 80 cm gold drain exists at none of the partners.

### Inferences
- For the three concealed brass fittings together, Deutsches Haus is the cheapest new source in one matching matt finish (1,450 + 650 + 610 = 2,710 ₾ before the card); with the list's 20% that family would undercut every family in the present budget, but the 20% is the list's wording, not a price printed on the shop's site.
- Cavoli and Nova can be struck off for concealed brass fittings: what they call gold is exposed or deck-mounted, which the page text alone would not have shown — it took their pictures to tell.
- A VitrA „ოქროსფერი“ item (code ending …23) is glossy gold, „სოფტ ოქროსფერი“ (…74) a softer gold and „ბრაშირებული ოქროსფერი“ (…25) brushed gold; VitrA's own gold shower set mixes the first two, so even inside one brand the tones should be compared in the showroom.
- Real tubular (column) radiators 1800 mm do exist online at Thermocenter (GZ2, GZ4, Warma Ar) and Mosaics (Irsap Tesi); the present budget note that none could be found is out of date.
- A 600×400 hall panel costs 89–95 ₾ at Thermocenter against the budget's ECA at 126.63 ₾ — a different make of the same size, not the same product.
- Mosaics is a partner the owner named himself, but everything it sells for these rows is euro-pegged and lands at 1.3–5 times the mainstream GEL price; only its shower set (1,860.03 ₾) falls inside the range already in the budget.
- For the wall tile the in-tone, in-stock range at the new sellers is 29.50–70.80 ₾/m² (Nova, Modus, Lussoni, Veneto Design), the same band as the budget's Domino and Jaokeni options; VitrA and Mosaics sit at 114–117 ₾/m².
- A light terrazzo with genuinely coloured chips under 100 ₾/m² was found only at Nova (PAT601M, on a darker greige base); the close colour matches cost 116.62–185 ₾/m² (Mosaics Blend Dots, VitrA Flake Light Greige, Veneto's Nuances Avorio Coccio).
- Veneto Design's Mirtak pair (1,350 + 785 = 2,135 ₾) costs about the same as Deutsches Haus's Deante shower and basin mixer (1,450 + 650 = 2,100 ₾), but Veneto has no matching hygienic shower.

### Gaps
- Lego Room and Praktika could not be read at all without a browser (no site found, Facebook returns only the page title), so what they carry and at what price is unknown.
- Veneto Design's site does not say which collections get 15% and which 30%, or whether the Mirtak fittings are in the „selected collection“ at all.
