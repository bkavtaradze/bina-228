All prices are GEL as shown on 2026-10-10 on the product's own page (or, for technoboom.ge, in the shop's own data feed that its page renders). "Sale / regular" is given where the page shows both. ## Which shops in Georgia sell built-in appliances online with prices?

### Takeaway
Eleven sellers publish prices for built-in kitchen appliances (megatechnica.ge, ee.ge, extra.ge with ~20 sellers inside, metromart.ge, kontakt.ge, technoboom.ge, technoshop.ge, gorgia.ge, kuppersberg.ge, midea.ge, mstore.ge); teka.ge and electrolux.ge are catalogues without prices, and alta.ge, vendoo.ge and veli.store could not be read. The market is far more concentrated than the number of shop names suggests: several "sellers" on extra.ge are the same companies with the same prices.

### Inferences
- "EXTRA MALL" on extra.ge carries ee.ge's assortment at identical prices to the tetri on dozens of models (e.g. Gorenje GE690X 699.99 on both, Bosch SPV2HMX03Q 2049.99 on both), and iMart.ge follows ee.ge within 0.01–10 GEL; "Megatechnica", "METROMART", "Kontakt Home", "Midea" and "Kuppersberg" on extra.ge are those shops' own stores. If the card is counted from the regular price, it beats the site's own sale only for the tap, the hob and the hood; for the oven the site sale (998.00) is already below 15% off regular (1188.30).
- With the card applied to the sale price the Krogh KH 604 B (1316.65) would still be dearer than the Gorenje GCE691BSC at ee.ge (1099.99).
- Megatechnica's 10% on large appliances would matter most for rows where megatechnica is already the cheapest (e.g. Elikor block hood 279.00 → 251.10, my arithmetic).

### Gaps

- Teka prices for all six items: not published; must be requested by phone or in the showroom.
