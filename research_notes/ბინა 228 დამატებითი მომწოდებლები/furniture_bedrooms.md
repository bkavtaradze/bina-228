# Bedroom furniture and mattresses — m² Club Card partners and other sellers the first round never looked at (Tbilisi, prices read 10 October 2026)

How to read this file. Every price below was read today from the product's own page or from the shop's own catalogue data that the page loads (saved copies are in `scratchpad/round2/pages/furniture_bedrooms/`). No browser was started. Prices are in GEL exactly as shown; "was" = the crossed-out regular price on the same page. The m² Club Card discount is **not** applied anywhere — it is listed separately. Designer's pictures used for judging: bedroom 1 — cream bouclé-like bed with a rounded soft headboard and a plinth to the floor, a cream round open bedside table with a shelf, a brick-coloured round pouf on wooden block legs; bedroom 2 — two single beds in taupe/greige velvet with tall, vertically channelled headboards on a low plinth, a small light bedside table between them, two chairs with a slim wooden frame and cream upholstered seat and back.

Machine-readable options (57 entries): `scratchpad/round2/candidates/furniture_bedrooms.json`.

## What do the m² Club Card partners carry for each item, at what price, what discount does the card give, and which publish prices online?

### Takeaway
Five partners publish real, per-variant prices online and between them cover every bedroom item: **Laboratory My Home / KARE / Sleep & Bed (all three on icrhome.ge, card 10%)**, **Ashley HomeStore (20%)**, **Comforter (20%)**, **Marco (25% on the m² list, 20% on Marco's own page)** and **Andazi (20%, mattresses only)**; the Georgian maker **Kave (10%)** publishes a full made-to-order tariff. Vibrant Home, Eurostyle, Verona, Pillow, Enza Home, Livin and Room publish no prices (showroom / message only), and Chillout and Interiorismo publish prices but not for our sizes or style.
